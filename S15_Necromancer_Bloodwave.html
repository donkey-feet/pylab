Equipment Notes

Starter Gear

  • Early on in your progression, focus on stacking Maximum Life to survive and essence regeneration, resource cost reduction to manage resources.
  • Having essence regeneration, resource cost reduction on your gear pieces is very important for this build to function properly.
  • Gamble pants to acquire Kessime's Legacy. They also drop from Andariel's.
  • Once you get a good Kessime's Legacy, the true power of the build will be revealed! Kessime's Legacy doubles your damage by creating two waves per cast from both sides, also automates pulling monsters in.
  • Juggernaut's Aspect is imprinted on chest. This is one of the aspects that gives the most toughness. And it is up by default without having to activate via any skill rotation.
  • Aspect of Hardened Bones on helmet for additional damage reduction and corpse generation.
  • Tidal Aspect is imprinted on boots to increase OP stacks, later on when you get Red Blessing, extra OP stacks increases your damage.
  • Early on resource management can be an issue since Blood Wave as a core skill is very resource hungry. Aspect of Deluge will mostly solve your early on resource issues. This aspect is very important to be able to smoothly play this build.
  • Gar generates additional critical strike chance, and Teb automates Iron Maiden application.

Stat Requirements for a Smooth experience:

3x Tempers of "Lucky Hit Chance: Restore Resource" on your jewelry.
10 - 12 Essence Regeneration
>18% Resource Cost Reduction

>50% Attack speed with ferocity stacks. You get a lot of Cap 2 attack speed from Bone Prison in this build.

Skill Rotation

Skill Rotation

  • You can literally play this build just pressing Blood Wave and nothing else. But there are few keys thing you can do to improve your rotation especially for teleporting and speed.
  • Use Sever to teleport near monsters. It also generates a corpse, you also consume corpses via Blood Mist to activate Flesh Eater legendary node but also continue teleporting via Blood Rush.
  • Cast Blood Lance from range to consume OP stacks via Overpower. When as you cast Blood Wave via Overpower modifier you get OP stacks back. In order to "gain" OP stacks, you need to spend some first if you are at the max capacity. This activates multipliers fom Bloodbath Legendary node and the Dominate glyph.
  • Main rotation is just teleport teleport! And spam Blood Wave.
  • Cast Bone Prison to gain extra attack speed. You may not even need to since Blood Wave when fully locked in one shots most t12 stuff but if you are fighting tankier targets and they are able to survive your multiple hits, Bone Prison increases your DPS significantly.
  • Orbs are automatically consumed by Iron Maiden and you don't even have to manually cast this as this is automated by Teb rune. Iron Maiden applies vulnerable.
  • Occasionally, weave in Reap to get essence back if your resources are depleted.
  • Pull is also automated Blood Wave combined with Kessime's Legacy. Enjoy true one button playstyle!
    Teleport Rotation
  • Spam Sever to teleport around, it ports you to a targeted location and creates a corpse.
  • Blood Mist consumes that corpse and also teleports you. So you rotate between these two skill as Blood Mist comes off cooldown.

Equipment Notes

Midgame Gear

  • As you upgrade your gear to Uniques, Ancestrals and reach higher Paragon, your power surges rapidly. Make sure to focus on defensive stats (esp. Max Life) in order to survive progressing into higher difficulties.
  • Until you get Ring of Starless Skies, resource management won't be as smooth. There are few things you can do to mitigate this; prioritize your gear with resource cost reduction, essence regeneration and LHC to regen essence. You can also try running Mages with Gift of Death upgrade.
  • Aspect of Deluge is a must have until you get Ring of Starless Skies. This aspect will mostly solve your early on resource issues.
  • Juggernaut's Aspect is imprinted on chest. This is one of the aspects that gives the most toughness. And it is up by default without having to activate via any skill rotation.
  • Aspect of Hardened Bones on helmet for additional damage reduction and corpse generation.
  • Tidal Aspect is imprinted on boots to increase OP stacks, this significantly increase your damage when combined with ⬍Banished Lord's Talisman and ⬍Red Blessing. You can also run these interchangeably between your amulet and charm slot; however, Banished Lord's Talisman has better affixes.
  • If you are still having defensive issues, Embattled Aspect is a great choice as a transfiguration out come on your amulet instead of Exploiter's Aspect. If you are still feeling squishy Endurant Faith is your go to option for extra tankiness.

Equipment Notes

Endgame Gear

  • Once you craft your mythics, this build will gain massive power and playability
  • Prioritize crafting Ring of Starless Skies as it will solve most of your resource issues, and increase your damage.
  • Juggernaut's Aspect is imprinted on chest. This is one of the aspects that gives the most toughness. And it is up by default without having to activate via any skill rotation.
  • Aspect of Hardened Bones on helmet for additional damage reduction and corpse generation.
  • Ideally, you want to imprint Embattled Aspect aspect on your boots and transfigure Tidal Aspect on your amulet using Kullean prism. However, it depends on which transfiguration you get first on your amulet. You can swap the aspects interchangeably either Embattled on your boots and Tidal on your amulet, or vice versa.
  • Embattled Aspect makes your fortify healing instant (it is a bug). If you are still feeling squishy Endurant Faith is your go to option for lazier gaming and farming!Embattled Aspect combined with Endurant Faith makes you super tanky, fully built character can feel quite immortal with this combination.
  • Tidal Aspect increases OP stacks, this significantly increase your damage when combined with ⬍Banished Lord's Talisman and ⬍Red Blessing.

Equipment Notes

Push Gear

  • Push variant of this build is optimized around more damage and defense and built slightly differently.
  • LHC to restore essence tempers on both of your rings sufficient enough to manage resources. So most of the essence regen related affixes on chest and boots are replaced with defensive affixes.
  • You imprint Aspect of Disobedience on your helmet for whopping 45% damage reduction. You activate this DR using Blood Lance.
  • On chest, you imprint Aspect of Glynn's Anvil instead of Juggernaut's Aspect.
  • Keeping up your Resolve stacks is extremely important to increase your survivability. ⬍Aspect of Glynn's Anvil gives you DR by stacking more resolve. At 13 stacks it provides 40% damage reduction. When monsters are not dying too easily Decrepify will build up stacks via Lucky hit procs.
  • Damage aspects in the pushing setup is also slightly different. Blood-Mad Idol makes sure Aspect of Untimely Death is up since you lose HP and heal constantly.
  • Blood-Mad Idol makes surviving more of a challenge but damage gain is so significant that it is worth it.
  • This setup needs to reach 100% block chance to activate Aspect of Redirected Force. which is a big damage multiplier. You need 20 resolve stacks and you achieve 100% block chance by transfiguring your amulet using kullean prism and adding Aspect of Interdiction. Combined with 20% block chance from shield this gets you to 100% block chance.
  • Tidal Aspect is imprinted on boots to increase OP stacks, this increases your damage fromRed Blessing.

Skill Rotation

Push Variant Skill Rotation

  • Cast Blood Lance from range before approaching packs. It is utilized to activate the second portion of the Bloodbath legendary node as you need to spend overpower stacks and gain to activate the second part. It also activates Aspect of Disobedience to get additional 45% dr.
  • Cast Bone Prison before packs can even get to you. Prison walls around you also provides additional protection versus certain enemies but more importantly it provides significant amount of attack speed. Cast Bone Prison whenever it is off cooldown.
  • Pull initial small packs at first and start casting Blood Wave. This will automate your curse application via runes and start generating resolve stacks. It should quickly reach 10 Resolve stacks. IMPORTANT: Decrepify is how you generate Resolve stacks via Resolve modifier. But when you start your pit/tower run, you have no stacks. If you engage big packs you will insta die. You need to make sure you make a small initial pull to max out your stacks before fully pushing rest of the dungeon.
  • As you cast Blood Wave, Overpower will increase and maintain your OP stacks at 11 at all times, completely eliminating the need to generate Blood Orbs. Who would have thought Blood Wave can be played without any orb generation!
  • CastReap to generate a corpse. Occasionally, weave in ⬍Reap to generate corpses
  • Once a corpse generated, cast Corpse Tendrils to apply vulnerable. Jaws Of Death variant makes the cast instant and it consumes corpses which activates Flesh Eater paragon node to get 60%x damage multiplier.
  • Blood Mist is your source of unstoppable, and movement. It also consumes a corpse to activate Flesh Eater. Try to cast this every 8 seconds or so, or when there is danger nearby to quickly get out of pressure.
  • Orbs are automatically consumed by Iron Maiden and you don't even have to manually cast this as this is automated by Teb rune.
  • Decrepify is auto applied via Wat rune.

FAQ & Mechanics
Mechanics

Blood Wave

  • C variant of the Blood Wave turns Blood Wave into a Core skill from Ultimate skill, removing it's cooldown and adding a resource cost. This changes the way this build has been played and built entirely from previous seasons.

Blood orbs

  • Special to Necromancer, with certain interactions, it can give you overpower stacks and healing.

Read up more on Attack Speed in our dedicated guide by Ava.

Resource Management
  • Early in your progression resource management can feel rough, make sure you have essence regen on your gear, especially boots and chest. You also rely on LHC to get some resources back via LHC to restore essence tempers on your rings and amulet. If you are still having essence issues, you can also run resource cost reduction affixes on your gear.
  • Once you get Ring of Starless Skies, resource management will be much easier.
How to Survive T12

T12 is full of one shot mechanics, and a lot of classes struggle to farm t12 without worrying about deaths.

  • Endurant Faith is a great item to get tanky. If you are still having issues, combine with Embattled Aspect as a transfiguration option on your amulet to have massive fortify generation and healing.
