Necromancer Blood Wave Starter Build

Starter Gear & Notes

  • Early on in your progression, focus on stacking Maximum Life to survive and essence regeneration, resource cost reduction to manage resources.
  • Having essence regeneration, resource cost reduction on your gear pieces is very important for this build to function properly.
  • Gamble pants to acquire Kessime's Legacy. They also drop from Andariel's.
  • Once you get a good Kessime's Legacy, the true power of the build will be revealed! Kessime's Legacy doubles your damage by creating two waves per cast from both sides, also automates pulling monsters in.
  • Juggernaut's Aspect is imprinted on chest. This is one of the aspects that gives the most toughness, and it is up by default without having to activate via any skill rotation.
  • Aspect of Hardened Bones on helmet for additional damage reduction and corpse generation.
  • Tidal Aspect is imprinted on boots to increase OP stacks, later on when you get Red Blessing, extra OP stacks increases your damage.
  • Early on resource management can be an issue since Blood Wave as a core skill is very resource hungry. Aspect of Deluge will mostly solve your early on resource issues. This aspect is very important to be able to smoothly play this build.
  • Gar generates additional critical strike chance, and Teb automates Iron Maiden application.

Stat Requirements for a Smooth Experience:

  • • 3x Tempers of "Lucky Hit Chance: Restore Resource" on your jewelry.
  • • 10 - 12 Essence Regeneration.
  • • >18% Resource Cost Reduction.
  • • >50% Attack speed with ferocity stacks. You get a lot of Cap 2 attack speed from Bone Prison in this build.

Skill Rotation

  • You can literally play this build just pressing Blood Wave and nothing else. But there are few keys thing you can do to improve your rotation especially for teleporting and speed.
  • Use Sever to teleport near monsters. It also generates a corpse, you also consume corpses via Blood Mist to activate Flesh Eater legendary node but also continue teleporting via Blood Rush.
  • Cast Blood Lance from range to consume OP stacks via Overpower. When as you cast Blood Wave via Overpower modifier you get OP stacks back. In order to "gain" OP stacks, you need to spend some first if you are at the max capacity. This activates multipliers from Bloodbath Legendary node and the Dominate glyph.
  • Main rotation is just teleport teleport! And spam Blood Wave.
  • Cast Bone Prison to gain extra attack speed. You may not even need to since Blood Wave when fully locked in one shots most T12 stuff but if you are fighting tankier targets and they are able to survive your multiple hits, Bone Prison increases your DPS significantly.
  • Orbs are automatically consumed by Iron Maiden and you don't even have to manually cast this as this is automated by Teb rune. Iron Maiden applies vulnerable.
  • Occasionally, weave in Reap to get essence back if your resources are depleted.
  • Pull is also automated Blood Wave combined with Kessime's Legacy. Enjoy true one button playstyle!

Teleport Rotation

  • Spam Sever to teleport around, it ports you to a targeted location and creates a corpse.
  • Blood Mist consumes that corpse and also teleports you. So you rotate between these two skills as Blood Mist comes off cooldown.
